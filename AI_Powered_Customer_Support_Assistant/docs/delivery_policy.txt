SHOPEASE DELIVERY POLICY

1. Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days. Express delivery takes 1-2 business days and costs $9.99. Orders placed before 2 PM ship the same day.

2. Free shipping
Orders above $50 qualify for free standard shipping.

3. Tracking
A tracking link is emailed once your order ships. You can also track it from My Orders in your account.

4. Delayed orders
If your order has not arrived within 10 business days, contact support and we will investigate with the courier.

5. Failed delivery
The courier attempts delivery up to 3 times. After that the parcel is returned to our warehouse and a refund is issued minus shipping costs.

6. Address changes
The delivery address can be changed only before the order is shipped.

7. Coverage
We currently deliver within the United States and Canada only.
