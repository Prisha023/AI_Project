SHOPEASE RETURN & REFUND POLICY

1. Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date, provided they are unused, in original packaging, and have the original receipt or order number.

2. Non-returnable items
Gift cards, perishable goods, personalised items, and opened hygiene products (earphones, cosmetics, underwear) cannot be returned.

3. Electronics
Electronics can be returned within 15 days of delivery. The item must be in working condition with all accessories. Defective electronics are replaced free of charge within 30 days.

4. Damaged or wrong item
If you received a damaged, defective, or wrong item, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos. We will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement or a full refund.

5. Refunds
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer.

6. Return shipping
Return shipping is free for damaged or wrong items. For change-of-mind returns, a flat shipping fee of $4.99 is deducted from the refund.
